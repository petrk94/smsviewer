Before you import: local data storage

Messages, SMS, contact details, photos, and other MMS media loaded into this app are stored locally in your browser profile. This app does not upload them or sync them to the internet.

Your data remains stored after reloading the page or closing the tab or window. The last completed import is restored when you open the app again.

You control when to delete this data. For sensitive information or a shared computer, use Delete stored messages and media on the main page before closing the window. Anyone with access to the same browser profile may be able to access stored messages.

Deletion removes the app’s stored import, including loaded media. Country and display settings are kept. Original XML files, downloaded attachments, and exports must be deleted separately. The app cannot guarantee secure erasure of disk remnants or backups.

Choose the backup’s default country

This setting is used only for local phone numbers that do not start with + or 00. International numbers keep their own country code.

Example: with Germany selected, 0176… can be matched with +49 176…. Choose the country where the phone was used when this backup was created.

You can change this setting later. Files and messages are processed locally in your browser.
